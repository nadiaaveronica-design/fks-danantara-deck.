TOPIC: Geography of Indonesia's animal feed industry: national production, mills/capacity, provincial/island distribution, clusters, corn & SBM usage, major players, Java-vs-Sumatra demand share proxy

SUMMARY: Indonesia's compound-feed industry is ~110 mills run by 44 companies in 10 provinces (USDA GAIN 2022-2026), with 81 mills (74%) on Java; the rest sit in North Sumatra, West Sumatra, Lampung, South Kalimantan and South Sulawesi. Installed capacity is ~30-31 Mt/yr (GPMT; USDA counts ~27.6 Mt poultry-feed capacity) against output that GPMT puts at 17.6 Mt (2022), 17.9-18 Mt (2023), ~19 Mt (2024 projection) and ~21.5 Mt (2025, +6.7% MBG-driven), while USDA reports 20.7 MMT (2024), 21.4 MMT (2025) and forecasts 23.3 MMT (2025/26) — the two bases differ and both should be cited. Poultry takes ~90% of feed; corn is ~48% of the ration (GPMT plans 43-48%) implying 8.3-9.7 Mt/yr of feed corn, and soybean meal is 20-25%, implying 5-6 Mt/yr SBM demand met almost entirely by imports (BPS: 5.33 Mt 2023, 5.43 Mt 2024, 5.54 Mt Jan-Nov 2025; USDA MY2024/25 record 6.1-6.2 Mt), with 60% of 2026 SBM imports assigned to an SOE. Corn imports are quota-constrained (1.28 Mt 2024/25, ~0.6-0.9 Mt thereafter). The main clusters are Banten/Serang-Cikande-Tangerang, Jakarta/Bekasi/Cirebon, Semarang-Demak-Grobogan, and the densest, Surabaya-Sidoarjo-Gresik-Pasuruan-Jombang (East Java alone claims 32% of national feed output and 33% of layers), plus Medan/Deli Serdang and Lampung/Tanjung Bintang (1.96 Mt capacity) on Sumatra, and Makassar/Banjarmasin in the east where new capacity is being directed. Major players: CPIN (10 mills, 10 provinces), Japfa (16 mills), Malindo (5 mills, 1.3 Mt), CJ, New Hope, De Heus, Wonokoyo, Sinta Prima. Triangulating mill location (74% Java), broiler population (63% Java) and layer population (Jatim+Jateng 46%), a defensible deck estimate is Java ~65-74%, Sumatra ~12-20%, Kalimantan/Sulawesi/others ~8-15% of feed demand and therefore of corn/SBM import discharge demand; label it as an estimate. Caveat: every page fetch was blocked by the network proxy and the search budget ran out, so all figures come from search snippets quoting the named sources and should be spot-checked against the USDA GAIN PDF, the GPMT deck and CPIN/Japfa annual reports before the deck is finalised.
