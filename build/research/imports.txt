TOPIC: Indonesia annual import volumes of core food & feed raw materials (wheat, soybeans, soybean meal, corn, sugar, DDGS, MBM, CGM, canola meal, fishmeal, rice), 2023-2026/27, world rank as wheat importer, wheat origins, and reconciliation of the deck's "~22 Mt" claim

SUMMARY: Indonesia's core food and feed raw-material imports are dominated by wheat (BPS: 10.87 Mt in 2023, 11.71-12.15 Mt in 2024, ~11.76 Mt in 2025; USDA MY basis: record 12.98 MMT in 2023/24, 10.45 MMT in 2024/25, 12.3 MMT GAIN / 13.2 Mt WASDE in 2025/26, 12.5 MMT in 2026/27), soybean meal (5.33 Mt 2023, 5.43 Mt 2024, 5.9 Mt 2025, 6.3 MMT forecast 2026/27, >95% South American), soybeans (2.27 Mt 2023, 2.68 Mt 2024, 2.56 Mt 2025, ~90% U.S.), corn (~1.4-1.5 Mt in 2024, collapsing to roughly 0.4-0.6 Mt in 2025 under the self-sufficiency policy with a 300 kt industrial quota for 2025/26), and raw sugar (5.07 Mt 2023, 5.31 Mt 2024, 3.93 Mt 2025 per BPS; GAIN 3.8 MMT 2025/26 and 4.0 MMT 2026/27; 2026 quota 3.1 Mt). Secondary feed ingredients add roughly 1.5-2 Mt: U.S. DDGS 1.02 Mt (2023/24) falling to 0.84 Mt (2024/25), MBM ~0.5 Mt (2023), CGM ~0.3 Mt, canola meal (India-sourced, tonnage not found). Rice was a 4.52 Mt import in 2024 but fell to ~0.36 Mt in Jan-Oct 2025. On rank: USDA's 2025/26 outlook places Indonesia at 13.2 Mt, marginally ahead of Egypt at 13.0 Mt, i.e. the world's largest (or joint-largest) wheat importer; in 2024/25 Egypt led at 12.0 Mt with Indonesia second at 10.5 Mt, so the deck should say "world's largest or second-largest". Origins: 2024 full-year BPS shows Australia 2.99 Mt, Canada 2.54 Mt, Ukraine 2.40 Mt, U.S. 0.69 Mt (Argentina ~1.8 Mt and Russia ~1.0 Mt by Sep 2024); in Jul 2025-Jan 2026 GAIN gives Australia 37.8%, Ukraine 18.3%, Canada 16.1%. The deck's "~22 Mt" is best supported as wheat + soybean meal + soybeans + corn + minor feed ingredients (DDGS, MBM, CGM), excluding raw sugar and rice: this yields ~21.2-22.9 Mt for CY2024 and ~20.6-22.3 Mt for CY2025, or ~22.4-23.5 Mt on a USDA MY2025/26 basis. Including raw sugar lifts the total to ~26-28 Mt, and including 2024 rice to ~32 Mt, so the deck should footnote the composition explicitly (recommended wording: "~22 Mt of wheat, soybeans, soybean meal, corn and other feed ingredients; ~26 Mt including raw sugar"). No source publishes an official aggregate, and all figures here were taken from search snippets because page fetches were blocked, so primary-PDF verification is still required.
