TOPIC: Geography of Indonesia's wheat flour milling industry: mill count, installed capacity, mill-by-mill locations and capacities, and Java vs Sumatra vs other-island share

SUMMARY: Indonesia's flour milling industry comprises 31 mills with 14.8 Mt/yr of installed wheat-grind capacity (USDA GAIN, April 2026), up from 30 mills/14.4 Mt in 2024 and 29 mills/10.3 Mt in 2014. USDA's island split is 24 mills on Java, 5 on Sumatra and 2 on Sulawesi (77%/16%/6% by count); no source publishes capacity by island. A bottom-up sum of publicly stated mill capacities (Bogasari Jakarta 11,600, Surabaya 6,000, Cibitung 2,600, Tangerang 500; Bungasari Cilegon 2,600; Harvestar Gresik 2,200; Sriboga Semarang 1,850; Cerestar Cilegon 1,500; Manunggal Cilacap 1,200; Wilmar Gresik, Fugui Gresik and Pundi Kencana ~1,000 each; Golden Grand 600; plus small Sidoarjo/Tangerang mills) gives Java ~34,700 t/day versus Sumatra ~3,400-4,500 t/day (Bungasari Medan 1,500, Cerestar Medan 500, Agri First 370, Wilmar Dumai ~1,000 unverified, Bogasari Medan undisclosed) and Sulawesi ~2,850-3,400 t/day (Eastern Pearl 2,250-2,800, Bungasari Makassar 600). That implies Java (incl. Banten) holds roughly 81-85% of national milling capacity, with a defensible deck line of 'about four-fifths of capacity and 77% of mills'; the total (~41-43k t/day ≈ 13.5-14.9 Mt/yr) reconciles with USDA's 14.8 Mt. The largest clusters are Greater Jakarta (~15,000 t/day, ~36%), Surabaya/Gresik/Sidoarjo (~11,000, ~26%), Cilegon/Banten west coast (~5,700, ~14%), Central Java ports Semarang+Cilacap (~3,050), Makassar (~3,100), Medan (~2,400+) and Dumai (~1,000). Bogasari alone is ~half of capacity and ~48-57% of sales; Cerestar (~12%) and Bungasari (FKS 40%-owned JV, ~6%) follow. Demand side: 2025 flour consumption 7.41 Mt (+5.5%), ≈10.0 Mt wheat, ~80% utilization per APTINDO; USDA sees MY2025/26 wheat use at 12.3 Mt (10.0 food, 2.3 feed), all imported, with 2025 imports running ~9-18% below 2024. Key caveats: every page fetch was blocked, so figures come from search snippets; mill capacities are of mixed vintage; and Wilmar Dumai and Bogasari Medan capacities remain unverified.
